## Product and engineering history

The versioned phase documents are living implementation records rather than beginner tutorials:

- [`v0.1.0`](v0.1.0) documents the foundation through Settings, including Seasons, Tasks, Habits, Diary, Constitution, Money, Objectives, Today, and Rank.
- [`v1.0.0`](v1.0.0) documents Season lifecycle, Money presets and Subscriptions, onboarding, account portability, self-hosted operations, and release hardening.

When current user-facing instructions and an older phase document differ, follow the current [README](../README.md), [user guide](user-guide.md), and [self-hosting guide](../SELF_HOSTING.md).
