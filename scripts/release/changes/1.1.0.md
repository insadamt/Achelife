Achelife v1.1.0 improves Money with account-backed debts, repayment tracking, and clearer financial activity.

## What's New

## Added

- Added payable and receivable agreements linked to People and Money Accounts.
- Added partial repayment history, due dates, forgiveness, reopening, and derived settlement status.
- Added debt-aware Transaction History and Account activity reporting.

## Changed

- Required every debt and repayment to use an Account so balances and financial history stay consistent.
- Advanced portable account archives to format version 2 while preserving format version 1 imports.
- Improved Money navigation, cards, category selection, quick actions, and mobile layouts.
